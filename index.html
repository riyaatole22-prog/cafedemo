Our Story

A small cup,
a big dream.

What started in 2014 as a tiny corner shop with one espresso machine and one stubborn dream has grown into a community of coffee lovers across the country. Goodluck Cafe was born from a simple belief — that a great cup of coffee can change your day.

Every bean is hand‑picked, every brew is crafted slow, and every guest leaves with a little more goodluck than they came with.
